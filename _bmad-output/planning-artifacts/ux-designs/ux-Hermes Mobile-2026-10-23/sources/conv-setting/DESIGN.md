# Design System: Fidelity Clean Light

## Brand & Style
Fidelity Clean Light follows a modern, professional, and accessible design aesthetic tailored for clarity, precision, and ease of use. It evokes a sense of reliability and refined elegance, utilizing generous whitespace and structured layouts to reduce cognitive load.

## Colors
- **Mode:** Light
- **Primary Color:** `#d1bc90`
- **Secondary Color:** `#6b5937`

## Typography
- **Headline Font:** Be Vietnam Pro
- **Body Font:** Inter
- **Label Font:** Inter

## Layout & Spacing
Built on a responsive fluid grid with consistent spacing intervals (`space-xs` through `space-xl`), ensuring predictable alignment and rhythm across form factors.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal layers and soft ambient shadows to establish clear visual hierarchy.

## Shapes
Rounded shape language with a base 0.5rem corner radius for friendly, approachable UI elements.

## Components
Designed for consistent interaction including buttons, inputs, cards, and chips.
